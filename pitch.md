# pitch
When I was in college, I didn't really know how to make friends. I could talk
to people, I could talk to my roomates a bit, but I didn't know how to ask
them to hangout or build deeper relationships with them. I was a nerdy kid that
wanted to stay home and write Haskell, and .. Not a lot of people was
interested in learning what a Monad was. So a Monad is a Monoid.. .. in the
category of EndoFunctors.

Talking to strangers was very difficult for me. It was intimidating. But I had
one goal in mind that required me to be able to talk to strangers: which is
getting a girlfriend. I kept going to random events, filled with strangers, and
whenever I would see a girl I'd want to talk to, I wouldn't be able talk to her
but I would make three new friends. I did that for about 4-5 years, and I'm
pretty good at it now. I mean I still haven't gone on a date with a cute girl,
but I'm really good at making new friends. And I thought about, what if there
was other people like me but less .. girlfriend motivated, right? They got
weird hobbies. Interesting things they want to do. But it's hard for them to
find similar people with some of the same interests. We're all just
disconnected now. Obviously, as a software engineer my idea is to fix social
problems by building an app. It's very simple, and it solves real problems.

Idea: maybe there's a theme that would speak to different people, IDK. I like
the adventurer quest theme because that's what connects to me.

The way the app works is like this:
1. You sign up into a message board filled with mutual friends of people you
   know or with similar broad categories of interests( for example "developers"
   ). If you have no friends at all, you can join a public board
2. Then, you just add ideas of things you'd already like to do. For me, it'd be
   studying Category Theory, learning Functional Programming, going on a new
   hikes and being with nature, going on a new hike, my standup comedy, working
   on a new plan with Claude Fable to get a girlfriend.. Each event maybe have
   a cap of how many people sign up
3. And then people would either sign up for your event and you go and
   coordinate and do it or you sign up for other peoples events
4. We would collect data about the events you're interested in / going to, and
   if you aren't going to events for some reason, a human moderator would come
   reach out and ask you why and talk to you. Having human intervention is
   important here because you can't always use robots to solve human problems.

And that's my app.
